Exam-season outdoor program

3× / week
Repeat this session 3 days per week. Keep roughly 1–2 reps in reserve and use controlled reps. With your joint history, stability and clean technique come before harder variations.
1. Negative pull-ups4 × 3–6

Step or jump to the top, then lower for 3–5 seconds. Think “elbows toward hips.”

2. Incline push-ups4 × 8–15

Use a bench or sturdy bar. Lower slowly. Progress by using a lower surface.

3. Pike push-ups3 × 6–10

Shoulder-focused pressing. Keep the movement controlled rather than forcing depth.

4. Inverted rows3 × 8–12

Pull toward the lower ribs with elbows relatively close to the body.

5. Dips3 × 8–12

Only use a stable setup and comfortable range. Skip if your shoulders feel unstable.

6. Reverse crunch3 × 12–15

Curl the pelvis toward the ribs. Stop if your lower back becomes painful.

7. Side plank2 × 20–30s / side

Brace gently and keep the body in one line. Progress time before adding complexity.

Progression

When you can hit the top of a rep range with clean form and ~1–2 reps left, make the exercise slightly harder: more reps → slower eccentric → pause → harder variation.
